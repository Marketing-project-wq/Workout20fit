# 20FIT — Home Photo Shot List

Reference for the 20FIT photo-sourcing team. These images fill the **Explore by
Goal** pills, **Explore by Type** pills, and **Featured Programs** cards on the new
Home page. Until they arrive, the app shows designed category-themed gradient
placeholders — so sourcing is **not blocking launch**, but real photos noticeably
lift the page.

## Rules
- **All photos must be 20FIT's own** — real gym, members, and coaches. Do **not**
  reuse images from iFIT or any other brand.
- Consistent look: clean, well-lit, energetic; leave darker/simpler area at the
  **bottom-left** of each frame (text label overlays there).
- Deliver web-optimised JP/WebP. Suggested min widths: pills ~800px, Featured ~1000px.
- Once ready, hand URLs to whoever maintains the CMS; they go into `_goalImg`,
  `_typeImg`, `_featImg` — **no code change needed**.

## Priority
**1) Featured (3–4) → 2) Explore by Type (6) → 3) Explore by Goal (4).**
Featured is the most visible block on Home, so shoot it first.
